**模块**: 指导性练习 (Guided Practice)

Reading and Discussion

Directions: Read the following passage and discuss the questions below.

1. What is the main idea of the passage?
2. What are the key points?
3. Do you agree with the author? Why or why not?

Sample answers:
1. The main idea is about the value of money in our daily lives.
2. Key points include: money is important for basic needs, but it cannot buy happiness or love.
3. Yes, I agree because while money is necessary for survival, true happiness comes from relationships and experiences.

Discussion tips:
- Use examples from your own life to support your opinions
- Listen carefully to others' viewpoints
- Try to find common ground in disagreements
