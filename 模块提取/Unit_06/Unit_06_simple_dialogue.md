Steve: Would you agree that Australian English and British English are very similar?
Alice: I'm not sure I quite agree.
Steve: Oh, what's your experience, then?
Alice: I'd just like to say that they're very different. It's true that Australians speak
English, but even people from Britain and America find it difficult to understand
their accent.
Steve: Well, you have a point there, but not all Australians speak with a strong accent.
Alice: Really?
Steve: Yes, you see, generally speaking, the more highly educated they are, the less
typically Australian their speech is.
Alice: Yes, you're quite right.
