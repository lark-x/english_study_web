Directions: Talk about your own personality with your friend and make some comments on his/
her personality. Use your own information. You may use the following tips to help you.
What kind of person do you consider ...to be?
He's (really) ...
What I really liked about him is ...
Do you enjoy ... in ...?
Did you know that he is ...?
