1. Facebook is a social networking service and website launched in February 2004. As of
December 2011, Facebook has about 845 million active users. Users must register before
using the site, after which they may create a personal profile, add other users as friends, and
exchange messages.
2. The lyrics "A smile is just a frown turned upside down, my friend" are from a soul song,
"Smiling Faces Sometimes", written by Norman Whitfield and Barrett Strong, which deals
with the subject matter of "back-stabbing" friends who do their friends wrong behind their
backs.
