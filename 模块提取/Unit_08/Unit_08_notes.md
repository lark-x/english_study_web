1. Attitude Is Altitude is Nick Vujicic's inspirational programme, which aims to help people
learn to overcome adversity, persevere under any circumstance and attain the height of their
ambition.
1. NeXT was an American computer company headquartered in Redwood City, California
that developed and manufactured a series of computer workstations intended for the higher
education and business markets. NeXT was founded in 1985 by Apple Computer co-
founder Steve Jobs. NeXT introduced the first NeXT Computer in 1988, and the smaller
NeXTStation in 1990. Sales of the NeXT computers were relatively limited, with estimates of
about 50,000 units shipped in total. Nevertheless, its innovative object-oriented NeXTSTEP
operating system and development environment were highly influential.
2. Pixar, an American computer animation film studio, began in 1979 as the Graphics Group, part
of the Computer Division of Lucasfilm before it was acquired by Apple Computer co-founder
Steve Jobs in 1986. Pixar has produced twelve feature films, beginning with Toy in
1995. The studio has earned 26 Academy Awards, 7 Golden Globes, and 3 Grammy Awards.
