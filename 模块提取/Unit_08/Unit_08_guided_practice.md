Directions: You come across an old friend at your university, who is supposed to pursue
study overseas. Express your pleasant surprise at the good news. Use the following tips when
necessary.
Oh, how marvelous (wonderful)!
That's good/splendid/wonderful news!
I'm so thrilled for you!
Isn't it fantastic?
You must be kidding!
I can't even believe my eyes/ears!
