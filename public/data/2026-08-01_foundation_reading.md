# Foundation Reading 001｜Small Improvements Are Easier to Keep

来源类型：原创练习  
用途：阅读、长句、翻译和短写作  
难度：四级到自考本科英语过渡

## English

Many adult learners stop studying English because they set a goal that is too large. They may plan to memorize hundreds of words, finish several textbooks, and practice speaking every day. The plan sounds impressive, but it is difficult to continue after a busy week at work.

A smaller plan is often more effective. Reading one short article, reviewing ten useful expressions, and writing five sentences may not feel dramatic. However, these actions can be repeated. After several weeks, the learner has built a collection of words, sentence patterns, and experiences that can be used again.

The key is to make progress visible. A learner can record the date, the material, the mistakes, and one sentence they want to remember. This record turns a vague feeling into evidence. It also helps the learner decide what to review next instead of starting from zero every day.

Technology can support this process, but it cannot replace the learner’s decisions. A program can schedule a review or show a progress chart. The learner still has to read carefully, recall information, and use English to express a real idea.

## 中文参考译文

许多成年学习者停止学习英语，是因为他们设定了过大的目标。他们可能计划背下几百个单词、完成好几本教材，还要每天练习口语。这个计划听起来很有气势，但工作忙了一周之后，就很难继续。

更小的计划往往更有效。读一篇短文、复习十个实用表达、写五个句子，可能不会让人觉得特别了不起。然而，这些行动可以重复。几周之后，学习者就会积累一批可以再次使用的单词、句型和经验。

关键是让进步变得看得见。学习者可以记录日期、材料、错误，以及一个想记住的句子。这份记录把模糊的感觉变成了证据，也帮助学习者决定下一步复习什么，而不是每天从零开始。

技术可以支持这个过程，但不能替代学习者自己的决定。程序可以安排复习或展示进度图表，但学习者仍然需要认真阅读、回忆信息，并用英语表达一个真实想法。
